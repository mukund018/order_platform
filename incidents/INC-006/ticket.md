# INC-006 - Out of stock, but there is stock

Reported by: Meera, category manager
Reported at: 10:31 UTC

Description:
This has probably been going on a while and we only just spotted it. When a
product gets down to its last unit, that unit never sells. The site says "out
of stock" and the checkout refuses it, but the stock report says 1.

I found eleven products sitting on exactly 1 unit right now. Some of them have
been on 1 for days. Across the catalogue that is a meaningful amount of stock we
apparently cannot sell.

Ordering two of something works fine as long as there are three or more. It is
specifically the last one.
