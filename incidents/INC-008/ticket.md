# INC-008 - Orders cancelling themselves

Reported by: Customer support + alert "HighErrorRate" (orders)
Reported at: 12:26 UTC

Description:
Customers are reporting that orders they placed successfully turn into
"Expired" a minute or so later. One customer watched it happen on screen - the
confirmation showed, she refreshed, and it said the order had expired.

We are also getting a trickle of 500s on checkout, maybe one in twenty, which
started at the same time.

It does not happen to every order. Quiet periods seem fine; it is worse when we
are busy. Stock levels also look off on a couple of the affected products but I
am not certain about that.
