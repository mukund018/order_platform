# INC-010 - Duplicate confirmation emails

Reported by: Customer support
Reported at: 14:03 UTC

Description:
Customers are getting the same order confirmation two, three, sometimes four
times. Same order number, same contents, arriving over a few minutes.

It is embarrassing but nothing seems actually broken - the orders themselves are
correct and only charged once, as far as we can see.

Started some time after the maintenance window last night. No errors in the
worker logs that support could see.
